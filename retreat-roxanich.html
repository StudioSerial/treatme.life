O retreatu About the retreat

Istra u proljeće i ljeto,
zakoračite izvan buke. Istria in spring and summer,
step away from the noise.

Tri vikenda u 2027. — travanj, svibanj i lipanj. Intimni Joga & Somatic Wellness vikend u prekrasnom Hotelu Roxanich, skrivenom ispod srednjovjekovne siluete Motovuna, Hrvatska.

Okruženi mirnim vinogradima i blagim brežuljcima doline rijeke Mirne, ovaj vikend osmišljen je da vam pomogne prizemljiti tijelo, utišati um i razmaziti osjetila.

Three weekends in 2027 — April, May, and June. An intimate Yoga & Somatic Wellness Retreat at the stunning Design Hotel Roxanich, tucked beneath the medieval skyline of Motovun, Croatia.

Surrounded by tranquil vineyards and the rolling hills of the Mirna River Valley, this weekend is designed to help you ground your body, quiet your mind, and indulge your senses.

landscape & stillness

Motovun i dolina Mirne kao prostor za integraciju. Jutarnje šetnje vinogradima, tišina koja zvuči drugačije kada ste zaista prisutni, zrak koji sam po sebi vraća u tijelo. Okoliš kao aktivni dio iskustva.

Motovun and the Mirna valley as a space for integration. Morning walks through the vineyards, a stillness that sounds different when you're truly present, air that brings you back into the body on its own. The landscape as an active part of the experience.

Design Hotel Roxanich smješten je u unutrašnjosti Istre, tik ispod srednjovjekovnog Motovuna. Prostori su nadahnuti talijanskom estetikom 1960-ih — custom-made namještaj, dizajnerske tkanine, prirodni materijali i crni mramor stvaraju atmosferu koja je istovremeno elegantna i topla.

Design Hotel Roxanich sits in inland Istria, just beneath medieval Motovun. Its spaces draw on 1960s Italian aesthetics — custom-made furniture, designer fabrics, natural materials and black marble create an atmosphere that is at once elegant and warm.
